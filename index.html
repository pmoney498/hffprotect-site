What we cover

Coverage for every stage of life.

№ 01

Mortgage protection life insurance

If something happens to you, your family gets money to keep the house, so the home you worked for stays theirs.

№ 02

Term life

Coverage for a set number of years, like 15, 20, or 30. The lowest cost, and the most protection for your dollar, during the years your family needs it most.

№ 03

Whole life

Coverage that lasts your whole life and never expires. Your payment stays the same, and it builds a cash amount you can borrow against if you ever need it.

№ 04

Final expense

A smaller policy that pays for the funeral and any final bills, so your family never covers those costs out of pocket.

№ 05

Indexed universal life (IUL)

Lifetime coverage that can also build cash over time, tied to a market index with a floor for down years. Growth isn’t guaranteed, and we’ll explain exactly how it works.

FAQ

Smart questions you should be asking.

Honest answers, in plain English.

Is Homefront Family Protection a real, licensed company?

Yes, a licensed independent insurance agency. We’re not an insurance company ourselves: the carriers issue the policies and pay the claims, and we compare them for you, always on your side. You can verify anyone who represents us in about a minute through your state insurance department’s website.

Are you my mortgage lender, or connected to the government?

No, and no. We have no connection to your lender, servicer, or any government agency. “Mortgage protection” is life insurance, the name describes the purpose, not a link to your loan.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. If you choose a policy, the carrier pays us. Your premium isn’t higher for it.

Do I need a medical exam?

No. The plans we offer skip the medical exam entirely, no doctor’s visit, no needles, no lab results. A few health questions, and everything else is verified securely and digitally through HIPAA-authorized checks with the MIB (Medical Information Bureau).

What if I’m older, or have health issues?

We work with older clients and serious health histories every single day, that’s our specialty, not the exception. With the number of companies we compare, we have a carrier connection for everybody: easier health questions, plans built for people who’ve been declined before, even options with no health questions at all.
