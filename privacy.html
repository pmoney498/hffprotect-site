Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026 · Last reviewed: August 4, 2026

Homefront Family Protection (“Homefront,” “we,” “us”) is an independent, licensed life-insurance agency. This policy explains what information this website collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it in plain English on purpose.

What we collect

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to submit through our quote-request form: your name, phone number, email address, state, and an approximate mortgage-balance range. If you later speak with a licensed insurance professional and choose to apply for coverage, additional information (including health information) is collected in that conversation and on the carrier’s application — not on this website.

Like nearly all websites, our hosting provider keeps standard technical server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability. This site does not use analytics tools, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies, and we do not set cookies of our own.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and have a licensed insurance professional contact you, consistent with the consent you give on the form.
  • To obtain quotes and, if you choose to apply, to submit your application to the insurance carriers you ask us to approach.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations as a licensed insurance agency.

What we share — and what we never do

  • Our licensed insurance producers: the licensed professionals who represent Homefront receive your request so they can contact you and shop coverage on your behalf.
  • Insurance carriers: if you ask us to shop coverage, we share your information with the carriers involved in quoting or underwriting the coverage you request.
  • Service providers: we use a customer-relationship-management (CRM) system and website hosting to operate; these providers process your information on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information when required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process.

We do not sell your personal information. Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Health information

We only collect health information if you volunteer it in the course of seeking a quote or application, and we disclose it only to insurance carriers for quoting and underwriting the coverage you request, or as you otherwise authorize.

Your choices

  • Stop texts: reply STOP to any text message from us. Reply HELP for help.
  • Stop calls or emails: email info@hffprotect.com and tell us how you’d prefer not to be contacted. We honor opt-out requests within 10 business days.
  • Access, correction, deletion: whatever state you live in, email us a verified request and we will honor reasonable requests to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, except where insurance law requires us to keep it.

Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, and we keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as insurance regulations require.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version here with a new effective date.
