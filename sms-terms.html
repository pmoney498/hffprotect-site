Text Messaging (SMS) Terms

Program: Homefront Family Protection SMS · Effective date: August 4, 2026

When you opt in on our quote form, you consent to receive text messages from Homefront Family Protection and its licensed insurance agents about your quote request and our life-insurance services — including responses to your inquiry, appointment scheduling and reminders, and follow-ups about coverage options you requested.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us.

Fees

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to cancel at any time. You may receive one final message confirming your opt-out. You can also email info@hffprotect.com. We honor opt-out requests within 10 business days.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or email info@hffprotect.com.

Consent is not a condition of purchase

You can receive quotes and buy coverage without agreeing to texts — just call or email us instead.

Privacy

Your mobile number and SMS consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details on how we handle your information.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
